# Catch the Stars

Stars are falling from the night sky. Move left and right to catch as many as you can. There's no way to lose: a missed star just disappears, and another one comes along. Every 10 stars you get a cheer, and the stars fall a little faster.

## How to play

- **Keyboard:** left and right arrows (or A and D). Space or Enter to start.
- **Controller:** left stick or d-pad. A or Start to start.
- **Phone, tablet or mouse:** tap to start, then hold your finger down and the player follows it.
- **F** toggles full screen and **M** turns the sound on or off. **Escape**, the **Menu** button, or holding **Select + Start** goes back to the game menu.

## Settings

The top of `index.html` has a "Settings you can play with" section: how fast the player moves, how fast the stars fall, how many fall at once, and the sky colours.
