# Find the Bunnies

Five bunnies are hiding in the bushes. Walk around the meadow and bump into bushes to search them. Every bunny you find hops along behind you. Find them all to see the bunny dance, then play again with the bunnies in new hiding spots. There's no timer and no way to lose.

## How to play

- **Keyboard:** arrow keys (or WASD) to walk. Space or Enter to start.
- **Controller:** left stick or d-pad to walk. A or Start to start.
- **Phone, tablet or mouse:** tap to start, then hold your finger down and the player walks toward it.
- **F** toggles full screen and **M** turns the sound on or off. **Escape**, the **Menu** button, or holding **Select + Start** goes back to the game menu.

## Settings

The top of `index.html` has a "Settings you can play with" section: how many bunnies and bushes there are, how fast the player walks, and the grass and flower colours.
